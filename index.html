Mission Briefing

Light cycles on the unexpanded Vic-20! You are presented with a multicoloured grid on which stranded people are located.

  • Objective: Collect all stranded men (stick figures) without crashing your Grid Bike.
  • Trail Warning: As your bike moves, it leaves behind a solid light trail. Running into your trail, outer walls, or obstacle blocks will result in an immediate fatal crash!
  • Easy Mode: Grid contains stranded men and empty grid tiles.
  • Hard Mode: Adds 10 (or more) dangerous black obstacle blocks placed by the computer across the grid!
  • Level Progression: Rescuing all men on the screen clears the grid ("GRID CLEARED") and advances to the next stage with more men and tighter challenges!
